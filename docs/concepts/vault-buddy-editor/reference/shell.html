Editor shortcuts

Start with the sample. Press Space. Record webcam adds a separate presenter layer. Save project keeps everything editable without rendering. Select a video clip, then use Fades in Properties. Import your own videos or audio into Media when you are ready.

Higher video tracks sit above lower tracks. Audio tracks mix together. Gold handles control fades. A crossfade blends two adjacent clips and shortens only their track by the overlap. Other tracks stay in place.

Every drag has a numeric alternative in Properties. Track buttons provide visibility, mute, solo and lock. Edits do not change original media. Browser rendering is for short reviews, not frame-accurate production export.
